# Time Tracking

**Tool:** Google Sheets  
**Status:** In Progress

## 1. Overview

The working hours of all team members are documented in a shared Google Sheets spreadsheet.

This allows us to track individual contributions and maintain an overview of the total time spent on the project.

## 2. Time Tracking Sheet

**[Open Time Tracking Spreadsheet](INSERT_GOOGLE_SHEETS_LINK_HERE)**

## 3. Documentation Guidelines

Each team member is responsible for regularly recording their working hours.

The following information should be documented:

- **Date:** When the work was completed.
- **Team Member:** Name of the person.
- **Activity:** Short description of the task performed.
- **Duration:** Time spent on the activity (in hours).

Activities include development, meetings with the client (AG) and team supervisor (TB), team meetings, documentation, research, testing, and project management.

## 4. Access

The spreadsheet is shared with all team members. Access for the team supervisor (TB) will be provided as required.

The time tracking sheet will be updated regularly throughout the project.
